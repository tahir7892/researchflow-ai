# Suggested live demo topics

Try a narrow, active research theme: `AI in higher education`, `electric vehicle battery recycling`, or `telehealth access in rural areas`. Request results since 2023. Source result counts vary by day and by topic and cannot be pre-guaranteed.

Public API queries are live. To reproduce a demo, record the exact date, topic, source statuses, and selected DOIs. Do not claim the retrieved sample represents all research on the topic.
